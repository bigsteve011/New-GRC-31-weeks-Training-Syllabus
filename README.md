# GRC Mastery Program

Welcome to the GRC Mastery Program repository! This project is designed to provide a comprehensive curriculum for mastering Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) over a span of 31 weeks. 

## Overview

The GRC Mastery Program is structured to enhance your understanding and practical skills in GRC frameworks, risk management, compliance, and auditing. The program is divided into seven phases, each focusing on different aspects of GRC, ensuring a thorough and in-depth learning experience.

## Structure

The program is organized into the following phases:

1. **Phase I: Foundations & Frameworks (Weeks 1-4)**
   - Introduction to GRC vocabulary, governance structures, and key frameworks such as NIST CSF and ISO 27001.

2. **Phase II: Cybersecurity Risk Management (Weeks 5-9)**
   - Focus on risk fundamentals, qualitative and quantitative assessments, and risk treatment strategies.

3. **Phase III: Compliance Frameworks & Regulatory Requirements (Weeks 10-14)**
   - Deep dive into various compliance frameworks and regulations, including GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS.

4. **Phase IV: Audits, Assessments & Reporting (Weeks 15-18)**
   - Understanding audit fundamentals, findings, remediation planning, and conducting a mock audit.

5. **Phase V: Asset Management & Identity Governance (Weeks 19-22)**
   - Covering asset management, identity and access management (IAM) concepts, and governance.

6. **Phase VI: Protect, Respond & Recover Operations (Weeks 23-27)**
   - Emphasizing security awareness, data loss prevention, incident response, and vendor risk management.

7. **Phase VII: Capstone, Portfolio & Career Launch (Weeks 28-31)**
   - Building a capstone project, preparing for a career in GRC, and interview preparation.
